## 📚 주요 이론별 원본 논문

### 1. Technology Acceptance Model (TAM)

**원본 논문**:

- **Davis, F. D. (1989)**. "Perceived Usefulness, Perceived Ease of Use, and User Acceptance of Information Technology". _MIS Quarterly_, 13(3), 319-340.
- **Davis, F. D., Bagozzi, R. P., & Warshaw, P. R. (1989)**. "User Acceptance of Computer Technology: A Comparison of Two Theoretical Models". _Management Science_, 35(8), 982-1003.

**검색 키워드**: `"Technology Acceptance Model" Davis 1989 original paper`

---

### 2. Self-Determination Theory (SDT)

**원본 논문**:

- **Deci, E. L., & Ryan, R. M. (1985)**. _Intrinsic Motivation and Self-Determination in Human Behavior_. New York: Plenum Press.
- **Ryan, R. M., & Deci, E. L. (2000)**. "Self-Determination Theory and the Facilitation of Intrinsic Motivation, Social Development, and Well-Being". _American Psychologist_, 55(1), 68-78.

**검색 키워드**: `"Self-Determination Theory" Ryan Deci 2000`

---

### 3. Theory of Planned Behavior (TPB)

**원본 논문**:

- **Ajzen, I. (1991)**. "The Theory of Planned Behavior". _Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes_, 50(2), 179-211.
- **Ajzen, I. (1985)**. "From Intentions to Actions: A Theory of Planned Behavior". In J. Kuhl & J. Beckmann (Eds.), _Action Control: From Cognition to Behavior_ (pp. 11-39). Springer.

**검색 키워드**: `"Theory of Planned Behavior" Ajzen 1991`

---

### 4. Cognitive Load Theory

**원본 논문**:

- **Sweller, J. (1988)**. "Cognitive Load During Problem Solving: Effects on Learning". _Cognitive Science_, 12(2), 257-285.
- **Sweller, J., van Merriënboer, J. J., & Paas, F. (1998)**. "Cognitive Architecture and Instructional Design". _Educational Psychology Review_, 10(3), 251-296.

**검색 키워드**: `"Cognitive Load Theory" Sweller 1988`

---

### 5. Growth Mindset Theory

**원본 논문**:

- **Dweck, C. S. (2006)**. _Mindset: The New Psychology of Success_. New York: Random House.
- **Dweck, C. S., & Leggett, E. L. (1988)**. "A Social-Cognitive Approach to Motivation and Personality". _Psychological Review_, 95(2), 256-273.

**검색 키워드**: `"Growth Mindset" Carol Dweck 1988 psychological review`

---

### 6. Diffusion of Innovations Theory

**원본 논문**:

**검색 키워드**: `"Diffusion of Innovations" Everett Rogers 2003`

- **Rothschild, M. L. (1999)**. "Carrots, Sticks, and Promises: A Conceptual Framework for the Management of Public Health and Social Issue Behaviors". _Journal of Marketing_, 63(4), 24-37.

## 📖 핵심 논문 읽기 전략

### 우선순위 추천 (시간 제약 고려)

**반드시 읽어야 할 핵심 3편**:

1. **Ajzen (1991) - TPB**: 조직 행동 변화 설계의 기본 프레임워크
2. **Ryan & Deci (2000) - SDT**: 내재적 동기 설계의 바이블
3. **Rogers (2003) - Diffusion of Innovations**: 혁신 확산 전략의 고전

**심화 학습용 3편**: 4. **Davis (1989) - TAM**: 기술 수용 측정의 표준 5. **Sweller et al. (1998) - CLT**: 교육 설계 최적화 6. **Fogg (2009) - Behavior Model**: 행동 촉발 설계

## 💡 실무 적용 팁

각 논문을 읽을 때 집중할 포인트:

1. **측정 도구 (Measurement Scales)**
   - 원본 설문 문항 그대로 활용 가능
   - 신뢰도/타당도 검증된 척도

2. **이론적 모델 다이어그램**
   - 변수 간 관계 시각화
   - 설문 구조 설계에 직접 적용

3. **실증 연구 결과**
   - 어떤 변수가 실제로 행동을 예측하는가
   - 효과 크기(Effect Size) 확인

4. **후속 연구 (Citations)**
   - 최신 확장 이론
   - 산업별 적용 사례
