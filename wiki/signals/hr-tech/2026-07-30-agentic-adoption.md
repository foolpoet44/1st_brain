## HR Execution Implications

### 1. Trust Ladder Verification (3 Stages)

**Stage 1 (Blind Faith) — PROHIBITED**
- "AI rejected, so rejected" → Legal liability under EU AI Act
- **Action**: Ban blind acceptance of AI recommendations

**Stage 2 (Distrust) — REQUIRED**
- "AI can be wrong" → Mandatory re-verification process
- **Action**: Implement human review for all AI rejections

**Stage 3 (Collaboration) — COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE**
- "AI judged this way. How do I verify?" → Hypothesis-driven verification
- **Action**: Frame AI output as hypotheses for human testing

### 2. Evolution Gate Specification

Agentic AI deployment requires 3-stage human gate:

```yaml
evolution_gate:
  required: true  # Human approval for model modifications
  audit_log: true  # Evolution history tracking
  rollback_enabled: true  # Human rollback authority
  validation_sample: 10  # Random N samples for human verification
  gates:
    - gate_id: EG-001
      name: "Modification Proposal Review"
      trigger: "Agent requests model change"
      action: "Human validates proposal validity"
    - gate_id: EG-002
      name: "A/B Test Statistical Significance"
      trigger: "Modified model deployed"
      action: "Human verifies statistical significance"
    - gate_id: EG-003
      name: "Quarterly Evolution Audit"
      trigger: "End of quarter"
      action: "Human audits evolution direction alignment with org values"
```

## Identity Shift: Guardian → Gardener → Translator

### Guardian (Past)
- Gatekeeper filtering unqualified candidates
- Blind Faith in AI recommendations
- Compliance as checkbox

### Gardener (2026-07-28 Insight)
- Cultivator of extendable identities
- Distrust-based verification
- Compliance as process

### Translator (2026-07-30 Insight)
- Reinterpreter who preserves original context while translating to fairer language
- Collaboration through hypothesis-driven verification
- Compliance as competitive advantage

**Core Principle**: "번역은 원본을 지우지 않는다. 검열은 지운다."
- AI recommendations: **Censor** (blind pass-through) → **Translate** (contextual re-interpretation)
- Bullshit tasks: **Censor** (eliminate without input) → **Translate** (voluntary delegation with worker input)
- AI signaling: **Censor** (hide AI usage) → **Translate** (transparent disclosure with education)
- Decision fatigue: **Censor** (individual blame) → **Translate** (organizational design failure)

## Evidence-Based Foundations

### Hybrid Fairness (CDP Metrics)

### Bullshit Tasks & AI Delegation
- Bullshitness ↑ 1 SD → AI delegation preference ↑ 0.39 points (β=0.39, p<.001)
- Bullshitness ↑ 1 SD → Required human agency ↓ 0.216 points
- **Implication**: Workers voluntarily delegate meaningless tasks to AI

### AI Signaling Effect
- AI signal condition: Organizational attractiveness M=6.05
- No signal condition: M=5.15 (d=0.80, p<.001)
- **Moderator**: AI self-efficacy (full mediation)
- **Implication**: AI signaling works only for high self-efficacy candidates; education needed for low self-efficacy

### Decision Fatigue Ecology
- Expected Utility Theory limitations
- Ecological Bounded Rationality (Simon, Gigerenzer)
- Challenge Theory (System 1 → System 2)
- Enactive Problem Solving
- **Implication**: Decision fatigue is organizational design failure, not individual self-control failure

## Pitfalls & Warnings

### ⚠️ Reliability ≠ Validity
- A system can have 100% reliability (consistent output) and 0% validity (correct output)
- **Always pair completion metrics with accuracy sampling**
- Example: Wrong classification with "100% success rate" is perfectly reliable but completely invalid

### ⚠️ Candidate Trust Gap
- 67% of candidates feel uneasy about AI interviews
- 72% share bad experiences online
- **Risk**: Brand damage if trust gap not addressed

### ⚠️ Legal Co-Liability
- *Mobley v. Workday*: Employers using AI tools may be co-liable with vendors
- Court ordered Workday to supply exhaustive list of employers (potential co-defendants)
- **Implication**: Vendor compliance is not sufficient; employer must audit independently
